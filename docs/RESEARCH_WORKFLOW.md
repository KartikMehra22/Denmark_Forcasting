# Day-ahead forecasting: research workflow and file guide

This guide maps the work to the supervisor's requests and explains how the data, experiments and results fit together. It describes the current local research workspace. The Git repository contains code and configuration; the `data/`, `results/` and `reports/` directories are ignored and must be supplied separately. An archived reproducibility package was prepared before the follow-up scripts were moved into `scripts/followup/`; commands within that archived snapshot use its original layout.

## 1. Scope and status

The study forecasts Danish day-ahead prices in bidding zones DK1 and DK2, at hourly and native 15-minute resolution. The previous-day price is the main benchmark. Other models are ridge regression, LightGBM, PatchTST and Chronos-Bolt; a price-only LEAR comparison is available hourly. Prices and errors are in EUR/MWh.

## 3. Data flow and availability

1. Historical hourly price extracts are `data/elspot_DK1_history.csv` and `data/elspot_DK2_history.csv`; native 15-minute extracts are `data/dayahead_DK1.csv` and `data/dayahead_DK2.csv`.
2. The retained original later-period API response bodies are `data/later_locked/DK1.json` and `DK2.json`, with `manifest.json`.
3. `scripts/day_ahead_study.py` joins historical hourly prices to hourly averages of the native quarter-hour prices. `scripts/controlled_study.py` retains genuine native quarter-hour prices for the 15-minute analysis. No hourly prices are upsampled into that series.
4. `scripts/rolling_completion.py` joins the frozen later data, constructs price contexts and target days, trains the rolling models and saves forecasts. `supervisor_processed_data.py` exports the normalized input series to `results/supervisor_response/processed/`.
5. Fresh historical API response bodies are under `results/supervisor_response/api_snapshots/`. They are **fresh retrievals**, not the original historical responses; `snapshot_comparison.csv` records the value comparisons against the retained extracts.

The current historical API snapshots contain delivery times and prices, but no observation-level publication or revision timestamp. The original historical response bodies were not retained and cannot be recreated as original copies.

## 4. Experimental periods and assumptions

All dates below are local delivery dates in Copenhagen and apply to both zones. Full input ranges are 1 January 2022–16 November 2026 for the combined hourly series and 1 October 2025–16 November 2026 for native 15-minute prices.

| Resolution | First training window for selection | Setting-selection period | Earlier test | Later test |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hourly | 29 Jan 2024–25 Jan 2026; 728 days | 28 Jan–25 Apr 2026 | 28 Apr–24 Oct 2026; 180 days | 25 Oct–16 Nov 2026; 23 days |
| 15 minute | 8 Oct 2025–25 May 2026; 230 days | 28 May–24 Jul 2026 | 27 Jul–24 Oct 2026; 90 days | 25 Oct–16 Nov 2026; 23 days |

The original weekly 15-minute training windows grow from 290 to 402 usable delivery days; hourly windows contain 728. Chronos uses its pretrained model with the last seven elapsed days of own-zone prices: 168 hourly or 672 quarter-hour values. Original hourly LEAR recalibrates daily on 729 eligible days; the matched weekly version uses 728.

The forecast is issued at 10:00 Copenhagen time on D−1 for delivery day D. It uses day-ahead prices for delivery through the end of D−1, assuming those auction prices were published on D−2. Nord Pool's normal publication schedule supports this timing, but the input snapshots do not establish availability or revisions at every individual cutoff. Two complete delivery days are conservatively excluded before each weekly fit because training targets contain up to 25 hours or 100 quarter-hours.

At daylight-saving changes, target days have 23/25 hours or 92/100 quarter-hours. The models produce up to 25/100 steps and are sliced to the actual day. Historical local-clock lag curves average repeated autumn slots and interpolate missing spring slots; Chronos context uses elapsed time.

Chronos's native recommended horizon is 64 steps. For longer quarter-hour days, the library recursively continues the quantile forecasts. Error by forecast step is descriptive: time of day and changing price volatility prevent attributing the pattern solely to recursion.

## 5. Additional experiments

- **Matched LEAR:** weekly hourly refits share the other models' origins and eligible training-date limits, but LEAR still has its own features and scaling.
- **Winter:** 1 December 2025–28 February 2026, 90 days. Settings were selected before the test in November 2025; the quarter-hour selection fit had only 22 usable days. Treat results as exploratory.
- **Spring:** 1 March–31 May 2026, 92 days at native quarter-hour resolution. Settings were selected in February using a first selection training window of 114 usable days. Spring weekly fit windows grow from 142 to 233 days. The original 2026 study and spring study do not have identical settings, so seasonal differences are not solely attributable to season.
- **LightGBM sensitivity:** candidate settings were scored on pre-test selection dates; the comparison was designed after the original results had been inspected. It is a post-hoc diagnostic, not an independent prospective test. Additional trees improved later MAE modestly but did not beat the previous-day benchmark in the four later-period cases.
- **Intervals:** each D forecast uses pooled scores from D−61 to D−2, excluding D−1, and the same rule applies to both resolutions. Earlier original calibration windows overlap the original setting-selection periods. The first spring calibration window overlaps 26 spring setting-selection days. Chronos is pretrained rather than locally tuned, but calibration independence and time-series coverage should not be overstated.
- **Statistical tests:** daily mean absolute-loss differences against the previous-day benchmark use a Bartlett HAC standard error with six lags and two-sided normal p-values. Holm adjustment is applied over the stated test family. `dm_tests_with_spring.csv` includes the added spring comparisons. The later test is only 23 days; avoid strong conclusions about rankings from that period.

## 7. Reproducing and verifying

Use Python 3.12, restore the frozen local data and results first, and install both dependency lists:

```sh
python -m venv .venv
source .venv/bin/activate
python -m pip install -r requirements-lock.txt -r requirements-supervisor.txt
python -m pip check
python scripts/followup/supervisor_verify.py
python scripts/followup/supervisor_spring_analysis.py
python scripts/followup/supervisor_tune_lgb_analysis.py
```

The original rolling experiment can be recomputed with `python scripts/run_completion.py`. The winter, spring and tuning forecast scripts retrain many models and write into `results/`; preserve a copy before running. The pretrained Chronos-Bolt-tiny revision is `a0e552de83495b5c28c14c71c374f3e33280b340`; the follow-up scripts expect it in the local Hugging Face cache. The archived package includes the pinned snapshot.

The recorded clean run used a fresh Python 3.12 environment and reran the original complete pipeline in an isolated copy. Both quarter-hour prediction files matched byte-for-byte; the hourly LEAR values differed only at floating-point precision. See `clean_run_verification.json` for all columns and hashes. Running a clean comparison again requires a separate completed rerun directory:

```sh
python scripts/followup/supervisor_compare_clean_run.py /path/to/clean/project
```

## 8. Items that remain limited

- No retained historical as-published price-vintage archive establishes every 10:00 cutoff or identifies all later corrections. Nord Pool operational messages demonstrate that delays occur, though the sampled delays resolved before the next morning's cutoff.
- Original historical HTTP response bodies were not saved. Fresh snapshots match the retained historical price values, but are not the original bodies.
- The 23-day later period is too short to establish a stable ranking. The winter and spring experiments are retrospective and separately tuned; the first spring calibration window overlaps selection dates.
- The cause of later LEAR and LightGBM deterioration is not proven. Input distribution shift was observed; a small LightGBM tuning search did not close the gap to the previous-day benchmark.
- The repository does not contain the ignored datasets, forecasts, checkpoints or figures. They must be shared separately in the reproducibility package if requested.
